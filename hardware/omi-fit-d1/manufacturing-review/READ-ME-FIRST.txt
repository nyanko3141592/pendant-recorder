SUPPLIER DFM REVIEW ONLY — NOT A PURCHASE ORDER OR MANUFACTURING RELEASE

Board: D1.1, 23 mm circular, nominal 0.8 mm, six copper layers.
Track/clearance: 0.10 / 0.10 mm. Through via: 0.35 mm pad / 0.15 mm drill.
No blind/buried vias requested. Exact stackup, panel, solder mask and assembly process require supplier acceptance.

The BOM contains catalog candidates, not qualified/reserved components.
The CPL uses KiCad's absolute XY coordinates, millimetres, and native rotations.
All 69 populated reference designators match the BOM and CPL. J3 and TP1–6 are bare PCB contacts and are intentionally omitted.
Bottom rotations and every polarized component must be checked in the supplier's assembly preview.
Do not infer assembly approval from a catalog number.

USB footprint: signal lands and locating holes retain their original geometry.
The two physical GND lands (four overlapping logical pad identifiers) are 0.60 x 1.12 mm rather than 0.60 x 1.15 mm.
GCT drawing USB4105 B4 gives 1.15 mm with PCB-layout tolerance ±0.05 mm.
This adjustment passes the project's 0.20 mm copper-to-hole rule, but supplier solderability/tolerance acceptance remains required.
See docs/d1-electrical-review.md and hardware/omi-fit-d1/usb-landpattern-review.json.
